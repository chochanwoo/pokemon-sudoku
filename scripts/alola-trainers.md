# Alola Result Ranks

The site's six result titles are a custom quiz hierarchy. Alola Champion uses
the player character Selene's portrait; Hau is a separate tier below Champion.
Elite Four portraits rotate between Acerola, Olivia, Kahili and Molayne (USUM).
Ace Trainer, Lillie and Alola Tourist are fixed.
Titles are shown without the Korean suffix "급" or the English suffix "tier".

The original S cutoffs are restored for Alola Champion, with A now named Hau.
B through E retain their boundaries. Saves contain attempts or points, so old results are
displayed with the new titles without rewriting progress or completed scores.
