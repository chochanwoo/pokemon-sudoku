# Alola Resort Panorama

- Tool: built-in `image_gen` (not the API/CLI fallback).
- Project asset: `web/src/assets/alola-resort.webp`.
- Original: `web/src/assets/alola-resort.png`.
- Output: 2172 x 724 PNG, copied unchanged from the generated output.
- Web delivery: same dimensions, WebP quality 88%, encoded by `scripts/optimize_resort.mjs` without cropping or compositing.
- Use: full-width home masthead. The Pokemon sprites remain separate, unchanged catalog assets.

## Final Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide scenic bitmap banner for an Alola-inspired Pokemon quiz hub. Create a bright, polished hand-painted anime game background of a tropical island resort, calm turquoise lagoon, a white beach, a small distant volcanic green island and a few low airy bungalows, palm fronds at far edges and a tiny coral-colored beach parasol. Relaxed, sunny, welcoming, uncluttered. Wide panoramic composition, 3:1 landscape, 1536x512 if possible. Designed to crop to a short full-width website masthead. The left third must be very pale clear aqua sky and distant quiet water with almost no detail, suitable for dark teal text overlay. Put recognizable palms, beach and resort buildings in the RIGHT half. Keep important scene detail in the middle vertical 60%. Soft flat painted shapes, crisp legible scenery, subtle texture, seafoam, lagoon cyan, deep palm green and tiny coral accents. Bright light background, no dark vignette, no photorealism, no gradients as decoration, no glow, no bokeh. NO text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no frame, no border, no people, no Pokemon or other characters. This is an atmospheric scenic background; actual Pokemon sprites are separate existing project assets.
